About the Journal

Journal Title  : Al-Khazin: Jurnal Pendidikan Agama Islam

ISSN               : 3110-4134

Frequency      : 2 Issues per year (June and December)

Language       : English; Indonesia

DOI Prefix     : 10.67864/al-khazin by Crossref

Indexing         : Google Scholar | Garuda | Copernicus | Crossref

Publisher        : Nawa Edukasi Nusantara

Al-Khazin: Jurnal Pendidikan Agama Islam is a scholarly journal published periodically, serving as a platform for disseminating research findings, theoretical studies, and critical insights in the field of Islamic Religious Education (PAI). The journal features articles relevant to the development of theory, practice, and policy within Islamic education. Its scope encompasses PAI curriculum development; PAI instructional design; PAI teaching strategies, methods, and media; evaluation and assessment in PAI; professional development for PAI teachers; technological innovations in PAI instruction; contemporary Islamic education; the psychology of Islamic education; and Islamic education management. The journal is published biannually (in June and December).
